# 双语写作与语感指南 / Bilingual Syntax and Style Guide

本指南用于在多语言环境（中/英）下生成、改写和润色文本。中文部分使用了地道的“意合（Parataxis）”短句，英文部分使用了地道的“形合（Hypotaxis）”结构与朴素英语（Plain English）。 在生成对应语言时，请直接模仿该语言段落的语感、句法与逻辑节奏。

This guide dictates text generation, rewriting, and refinement in bilingual (ZH/EN) contexts. The Chinese section employs authentic "parataxis" (implicit short clauses), while the English section utilizes authentic "hypotaxis" (structured clauses) and Plain English. When generating text in either language, strictly mimic the rhythm, syntax, and logical flow demonstrated in its respective section.

---

## 1. 动词发力与词语归位 / Active Verbs and Word Fit

**[ZH] 动词发力，拒绝名词化** 中文靠动词发力。找出真正的动作，直接让它作谓语。删去“进行”、“实现”、“完成”这类空心的万能词。把动作从名词的壳子里剥出来，句子自然干脆。词要配位，只有实体会崩塌，抽象概念不会。

**[EN] Active Verbs, Avoid Nominalization** English relies on active verbs. Find the true action, and make it the main predicate. Remove hollow verbs like "perform," "achieve," or "conduct." Strip the action out of heavy noun phrases, and the sentence will naturally become crisp. Ensure words fit their subjects logically.

## 2. 句子节奏与修饰秩序 / Sentence Rhythm and Modifiers

**[ZH] 短句推进，修饰前置** 中文的美感在于短句串珠。顺着逻辑，把长句拆成逗号连接的短句。一节一节推进事实。把修饰成分放在名词前面，事情说清楚再落脚。不要用破折号拖着长串的补充说明。

**[EN] Natural Flow, Clauses After** English flows through structured clauses. Build the rhythm with relative clauses (who, which, that), rather than chopping ideas into fragmented commas. Attach descriptive details after the noun. Deliver the main point clearly, without trailing off into unnatural phrasing.

## 3. 逻辑连结与留白 / Logical Cohesion and Transitions

**[ZH] 隐式连结，克制虚词** 中文讲究意合，逻辑藏在先后顺序里。删掉不必要的“从而”、“进而”、“基于”。上下文意思连得上，就不需要硬塞连词。事实讲完，句子自然结束。

**[EN] Precise Conjunctions, Avoid Bloat** English requires explicit structural cohesion, but it must remain lean. Use precise conjunctions to link clauses. Remove bloated transition phrases like "It is important to note that" or "Furthermore." Once the logic is connected, let the sentence end.

## 4. 事实先行与去标签化 / Concrete Details and De-jargonization

**[ZH] 具体先行，拒绝空泛** 先给细节，再出结论。用真实的动作和数据说话。找不到具体细节支撑的抽象判断，通常是废话。慎用“～化”、“～性”等抽象后缀。事物有几点就说几点，不要为了气势强行凑成三个。

**[EN] Concrete First, Reject Jargon** Provide details first, then draw conclusions. Rely on actual actions and data. Abstract judgments without concrete evidence are usually corporate buzzwords. Strip away dramatic AI vocabulary like "delve," "tapestry," or "seamless." If there are two points, list two; do not force a triad.

## 5. 表达姿态与防御性克制 / Posture and Defensive Restraint

**[ZH] 平视读者，事实讲完即止** 平视读者。事实讲完即止。不要在段末强行拔高，删掉“综上所述”、“值得深思”。下判断要干脆，删掉“当然也要看到”这种两头堵的免责废话。不谄媚，不自夸。

**[EN] Plain English, Stop When Done** Treat the reader as an equal. Once the facts are stated, stop writing. Do not inflate the tone, and drop meta-discourse like "In conclusion." Make firm judgments, and remove defensive disclaimers like "However, we must also consider." Do not flatter, and do not boast.
